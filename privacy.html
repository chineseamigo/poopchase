💩 Poop Chase

Privacy Policy · Effective November 12, 2026

Poop Chase is a mobile arcade game developed by ChineseAmigo. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game or contact us.

Information stored on your device

Poop Chase can save game settings, character customization, progress and cosmetic ownership information locally on your device to make the game work. You do not need to create an account to play.

In-app purchases

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment information. The game uses purchase and restoration status to determine which cosmetic items you own. We do not ask you to provide a payment card number directly to us. Apple's handling of purchase information is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem, device model or screenshots. We use that information only to reply and provide support. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.

Advertising and third-party services

The game's privacy disclosures must reflect the services actually included in the version distributed through the App Store. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added to the game, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices before that version is released.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by deleting the app from your device; certain purchase records remain with Apple and may be restored using your Apple account. If you email us, we retain the correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us at the address below, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children's privacy

Poop Chase does not require players to provide their name, email address, or an account to play. If a parent or guardian has a privacy question, they may contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as the game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
