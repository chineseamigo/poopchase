Flappy Frenchie

Need help with Enzo and Hugo's flying adventure? Find answers to common questions and learn how to request assistance.

Gameplay Help

How do I play?

Tap the screen to help your French Bulldog fly. Navigate between obstacles and aim for a new high score.

How do I switch between Enzo and Hugo?

Use the character selection option in the game menu, if available in your version.

Why does the environment change?

Flappy Frenchie features multiple themed environments that change as your score increases. The environment order can vary between runs.

How do I change the music or sound?

Open the game's Settings menu to adjust the available audio options.

Technical Support

The game won't open or crashes. What should I do?

Close and reopen the game, restart your device, and check for an updated version. If the issue continues, contact support with your device model, iOS version, game version, and a description of what happened.

I can't hear any sound.

Check the game's audio settings and your device's volume and Silent Mode settings.

An advertisement won't load.

Rewarded advertisements depend on availability and network connectivity. Try again later. If an ad is unavailable, you should still be able to play the game normally.

Purchases & Advertising

If your installed version includes a purchase to remove ads, use the game's Restore Purchases option to restore eligible purchases made with the same Apple Account. If you cannot restore a purchase, contact support and include the issue details; do not send payment card information.

Features may vary by game version. Apple's purchase and refund support is available at reportaproblem.apple.com.
